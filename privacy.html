Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The Standard is a private, internal business platform for agents of Grind Agencies Inc. and its affiliated agency, The Standard Financial Group ("we," "us"). It is not a consumer product and is not intended for the general public. This policy explains what the app and website (tsfg.app) collect and how we use it.

Who can use it

Access is limited to verified agents who sign in with a company-issued code and are approved by Operations. There are no public sign-ups.

What we collect

  • Account info — your name and company-issued agent code, used to sign you in and scope what you can see (your team, your pipeline).
  • Business data you enter — client financial-review inputs, policy and new-business records, appointments, notes, and messages you create in the platform. This is company business data used to run the agency.
  • Device push token — if you enable notifications in the app, we store an anonymous device token so we can send you work alerts (a new sale, a coaching nudge, a training reminder). It cannot be used to identify you personally on its own.
  • Basic usage/technical data — standard information your browser or device sends when loading a web page, used to keep the service working and secure.

How we use it

Only to operate the platform: authenticate you, show the right data, run team coaching and recognition, send work notifications, and keep records the agency needs. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Client information

Some information entered relates to the agency's clients (for example, figures needed to prepare an insurance recommendation or a required 1035 exchange form). Agents must only enter such information with a legitimate business purpose. It is stored securely and used solely to service that client and meet regulatory requirements.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) on secured cloud servers and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Sharing

We share data only within the agency according to your role and reporting line, and with service providers that help us run the platform (such as hosting and, if you enable it, Apple Push Notification service to deliver alerts). We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the agency and its clients.

Your choices

You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings. To request removal of your account or data, contact us at the address below. Note some records must be retained to meet legal and regulatory obligations.

Children

The platform is for licensed/appointed agents and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
