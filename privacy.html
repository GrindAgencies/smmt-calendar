Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The Standard app (“the app”) is the internal platform for the agents and staff of The Standard Financial Group. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The app is for authorized members of our organization and requires a valid agency code to sign in.

Information we collect

To operate the platform, the app stores the following about agents and staff:

  • Contact information — name, email address, and phone number.
  • Account identifiers — your agency code and role.
  • Work content you create — messages, notes, tasks, new-business entries, scheduling and training records, and similar activity within the platform.

The app does not collect your location, and it does not use advertising or cross-app tracking technologies.

Client information entered by agents

Agents use the app to record the business they write. That means the app also holds information about their clients — name, contact details, date of birth, and the policy details needed to service the business. This information is entered by the agent, is visible only to that agent, their upline and the operations team, and is used solely to service the policy and meet the agency's record-keeping obligations. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared outside the agency except as required to place business with a carrier or to comply with the law.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app registers a device token with Apple or Google so we can send you reminders about your appointments, trainings and team activity. The token identifies the device, not you personally, and is deleted when you sign out or delete your account. We do not use it for advertising.

How we use it

Information is used solely to provide the platform’s features — signing you in, showing your dashboards and tasks, enabling team communication, scheduling, and tracking business activity for your agency. It is used for app functionality only.

How it’s stored

Data is stored securely with our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts the database and services that run the app. Access is limited to authorized members of The Standard Financial Group.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Data retention & deleting your account

Information is retained while you are an active member of the organization.

You can delete your account yourself at any time — in the app under My Profile → Danger zone → Delete my account, or on the web at tsfg.app/delete-account.html. You will be asked for your PIN so that nobody else can close your account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your sign-in and PIN, every device registered for notifications, your email address, phone number and state, your calendar links and your warm-market list. You will not be able to sign in again and it cannot be undone.

What we have to keep, and why: your name and your position on the org chart, and the policies you wrote and the commissions attached to them. An insurance agency is required to keep the record of who wrote which policy, so those records remain after an account is deleted — exactly as they do when someone leaves the organization.

If you would prefer us to action it for you, or you have lost your PIN, email admin@grindagencies.com from the address on your account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to The Standard Financial Group operations team at admin@grindagencies.com.
