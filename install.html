Signing in the first time

  1. Enter your agent code — the one your upline gave you.
  2. Type your first and last name.
  3. Choose a 4-digit PIN. You'll use it from then on.
  4. To prove it's you, enter the email or last 4 digits of the phone on your record. No email or phone on file? Ask Operations for a setup code.

Trouble getting in? Message your upline or Operations.
